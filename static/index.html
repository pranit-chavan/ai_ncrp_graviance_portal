Examples of cybercrime in India

  • Payment fraudMoney taken through a deceptive link, UPI request, or bank transaction.
  • ImpersonationSomeone pretends to be a trusted person, company, or public service.
  • Account accessSomeone gets into your email, social media, or other account without permission.
  • Online harassmentThreats, stalking, blackmail, or repeated abusive messages online.

What may help when reporting

  • When it happenedAn approximate date and time are useful if you are unsure.
  • Transaction detailsAmount and reference number, if money was involved.
  • Relevant evidenceScreenshots, messages, emails, payment alerts, or links.
  • Where it happenedThe bank, payment app, website, or social platform involved.

Never share passwords, PINs, one-time codes, or full card details here.

About this project CyberAssist is the Intelligent Cybercrime Complaint Analysis and Category Classification System using NLP, an independent academic prototype, not a government service. It stores records in this project workspace and has no sign-in or access controls. Depending on configuration, complaint text may be processed by an external AI provider. Use fictional details only.
